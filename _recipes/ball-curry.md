Ball Curry

Ingredients

[ ] Minced meat (beef or mutton)

[ ] Onions

[ ] Garlic

[ ] Ginger

[ ] Garam masala

[ ] Turmeric

[ ] Tomatoes

[ ] Fresh coriander

[ ] Coconut milk

Method

Season minced meat with crushed ginger-garlic and spices, then roll firmly into walnut-sized meatballs.

Sauté finely sliced onions, tomatoes, and ground spices into a rich curry base.

Gently slide meatballs into the simmering gravy without stirring vigorously so they hold shape.

Simmer covered until meat is cooked through, then swirl in coconut milk and fresh coriander.

Cook's Notes & Pairing Tips

Traditional Anglo-Indian recipe; pairs wonderfully with yellow coconut rice or warm crusty bread.
