Chicken-Mushroom Sausage Rolls with Spicy Tomato Sauce
Ingredients
Sausage Roll Filling
[ ] 300g brown cup mushrooms, chopped
[ ] 3cm-piece ginger, peeled, sliced
[ ] 1 garlic clove
[ ] 1 tbsp vegetable oil
[ ] 1 leek, pale section only, thinly sliced
[ ] 1 tsp salt
[ ] 1/4 cup (60ml) thickened cream, divided (2 tbsp for filling, remainder for brushing)
[ ] 1 tbsp oyster sauce
[ ] 1 tsp roasted sesame oil
[ ] 1/4 cup (20g) panko breadcrumbs
[ ] 2 tbsp finely chopped coriander
[ ] 500g Coles RSPCA Approved Chicken Mince
[ ] 2 sheets frozen puff pastry, just thawed
[ ] 2 tsp black sesame seeds
[ ] 2 tsp white sesame seeds
Gochujang Tomato Sauce
[ ] 3/4 cup (185ml) tomato sauce
[ ] 1/4 cup (80g) gochujang sauce or chilli sauce
[ ] 1 tbsp rice wine vinegar
[ ] 1 tsp roasted sesame oil
Method
Start the filling: Preheat oven to 200°C (180°C fan-forced). In a food processor, pulse mushrooms, ginger, and garlic until finely chopped but not pureed. Heat vegetable oil in a medium saucepan over medium-high heat. Add leek and salt; cook, stirring, for 2 mins or until leek softens slightly. Add mushroom mixture and cook, stirring, for 10–12 mins or until browned and tender. Spread onto a baking tray and set aside to cool slightly.
Add the mince: In a medium bowl, combine mushroom mixture, 2 tbsp cream, oyster sauce, and sesame oil. Season. Stir in breadcrumbs and coriander. Using clean hands, gently combine chicken mince and breadcrumb mixture.
Make the rolls: Line a baking tray with baking paper. Place 1 pastry sheet on a lightly floured work surface and cut in half. Shape one-quarter of the mince mixture into a log. Place along 1 long edge of 1 pastry half. Roll up pastry to enclose filling. Cut into 6 pieces and place, seam-sides down, on the lined tray. Repeat with remaining pastry and mince mixture (to make 24 rolls total).
Bake the rolls: Brush remaining cream over rolls. Sprinkle with combined black and white sesame seeds. Bake for 30–35 mins or until golden and filling is cooked through. Set aside for 5 mins to cool.
Whisk up the tomato sauce: In a small bowl, whisk tomato sauce, gochujang sauce, rice wine vinegar, and sesame oil until smooth. Season to taste. Transfer to a serving jar.
Dip & enjoy: Place warm sausage rolls on a platter. Serve with gochujang tomato sauce.
Cook's Notes & Pairing Tips
Makes 24 cocktail sausage rolls. Cooking down the mushroom and leek mixture thoroughly is key so excess moisture doesn't make the pastry soggy. Allow the mushroom mixture to cool before folding into the raw chicken mince.
