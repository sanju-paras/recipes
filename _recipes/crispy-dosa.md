Crispy Dosa

Ingredients

[ ] Raw rice

[ ] Urad dal

[ ] Chana dal

[ ] Fenugreek seeds (methi)

[ ] Salt

[ ] Water

[ ] Ghee or sesame oil

Method

Soak rice, urad dal, chana dal, and fenugreek seeds for 5 to 6 hours, then grind to a fluffy, smooth batter with water and salt.

Ferment overnight in a warm spot until visibly doubled and aerated.

Heat a cast-iron tawa; rub with halved onion and pour a ladleful of batter, swirling outwards thinly.

Drizzle ghee or sesame oil along the circumference; cook on medium-high heat until golden and paper-crisp.

Cook's Notes & Pairing Tips

Pairs perfectly with Venn Pongal and brinjal chutney; wipe hot tawa with oil-rubbed onion before spreading batter for paper-thin crispiness.
