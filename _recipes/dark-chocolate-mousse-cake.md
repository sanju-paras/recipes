Dark Chocolate Mousse Cake

Ingredients

[ ] 180g packet chocolate wheat biscuits

[ ] 75g butter, melted

[ ] 1/4 cup strong espresso coffee

[ ] 2 x 250g Whittaker's 50% Dark Chocolate (chopped)

[ ] 600ml cream

[ ] Cocoa powder for dusting

Method

Grease and line a 20cm springform cake tin.

Place biscuits in a food processor and blitz until the mix resembles fine breadcrumbs. Add melted butter and blitz again until combined. Press mixture firmly into the base of the cake tin and refrigerate.

Place chocolate in a large heatproof bowl over a saucepan of simmering water. Heat until melted, stirring often. Stir in espresso coffee, remove from heat, and set aside to cool slightly. (Note: you can also melt chocolate in a glass or ceramic bowl in the microwave on high in 20-second bursts, stirring between each burst).

Beat the cream until soft peaks form. Gently fold the whipped cream into the cooled chocolate so as not to lose the airiness. Pour this mixture over the chilled biscuit base and smooth the top with a spatula.

Return to the fridge for approximately 3 hours, until set firm.

Dust generously with cocoa powder and serve.

Cook's Notes & Pairing Tips

Rich, no-bake Whittaker's chocolate mousse cake with espresso. Ensure the melted chocolate has cooled slightly before folding in the softly whipped cream to prevent it from seizing or losing its airy volume.
