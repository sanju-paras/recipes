Venn Pongal

Ingredients

[ ] Raw rice

[ ] Moong dal

[ ] Ghee

[ ] Cumin seeds

[ ] Black peppercorns

[ ] Cashews

[ ] Fresh ginger (finely chopped)

[ ] Curry leaves

[ ] Asafoetida (hing)

Method

Soak raw rice and moong dal for 30 minutes.

Pressure cook with 4 cups of water and salt until soft and mashable.

Temper cumin seeds, whole peppercorns, ginger, curry leaves, and cashews in generous hot ghee with a pinch of hing.

Combine the fragrant tempering thoroughly into the cooked pongal and serve piping hot.

Cook's Notes & Pairing Tips

Authentic family favorite; serve piping hot with fresh coconut chutney or homemade brinjal chutney.
