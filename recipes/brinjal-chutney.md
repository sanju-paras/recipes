Brinjal Chutney

Ingredients

[ ] Brinjal (Eggplant)

[ ] Ripe tomatoes

[ ] Shallots

[ ] Green chillies

[ ] Byadgi chillies

[ ] Tamarind extract

[ ] Sesame oil

[ ] Mustard seeds

[ ] Curry leaves

Method

Simmer chopped brinjals, tomatoes, shallots, and both chillies in a little water for 15 minutes until tender.

Allow the cooked mixture to cool, then grind coarsely with salt and tamarind extract.

Heat sesame oil in a small pan, splutter mustard seeds and curry leaves, and temper over the chutney.

Cook's Notes & Pairing Tips

Classic pairing specifically made for Venn Pongal; tamarind imparts delicious tanginess.
