Ammachi's Keerai Masiyal (Paati's Style)

Ingredients

[ ] 1 packet spinach

[ ] 1 piece tamarind

[ ] 1/4 tsp salt

[ ] 1/2 tsp cumin seeds (per packet of spinach)

[ ] 3 dried chillies

[ ] 1/4 tsp black pepper

[ ] 1 tbsp chana dal

[ ] 1 tbsp urad dal

[ ] 1 clove garlic (Amma's family style) OR pinch of asafoetida / hing (Paati's style)

[ ] 1 tbsp cooking oil

Method

In a pan, fry 1 tbsp chana dal and 1 tbsp urad dal in oil until light golden.

Add the cumin seeds, 3 dried chillies, black pepper, and garlic (or hing); sauté and fry ("vadhakify") until aromatic, then set aside.

Blanch/cook the spinach with the tamarind piece and 1/4 tsp salt for around 1 minute (keep the vibrant green colour).

Coarsely mash or blend the cooked spinach together with the fried dal-spice mixture into a thick, textured masiyal.

Cook's Notes & Pairing Tips

Family recipe from Ammachi / Paati. You can use either 1 clove of garlic (Amma's family variation) or asafoetida/hing (Paati's variation). Do not overcook the spinach—keep it to roughly 1 minute of blanching so it preserves its bright emerald color. Traditional Monday dinner pairing: serve alongside hot rice, dal, cabbage thoran, potato fry, and vada.
