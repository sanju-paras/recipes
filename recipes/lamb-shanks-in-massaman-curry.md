Lamb Shanks in Massaman Curry

Ingredients

[ ] 4 Lamb shanks

[ ] Massaman curry paste

[ ] Coconut milk

[ ] Potatoes

[ ] Onion

[ ] Fish sauce

[ ] Roasted peanuts

Method

Sear seasoned lamb shanks in a heavy Dutch oven until deeply browned on all sides.

Sauté curry paste in coconut cream until fragrant and oil begins to split.

Add browned shanks, peeled chunked potatoes, onion, and broth to the pot.

Slow simmer on low heat for 2 hours until the meat is tender and falling off the bone.

Cook's Notes & Pairing Tips

Liked by Julia; braise low and slow until meat falls off bone; serve with jasmine rice and crushed peanuts.
