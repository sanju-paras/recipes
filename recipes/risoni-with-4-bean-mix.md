Risoni with 4-Bean Mix

Ingredients

[ ] Risoni pasta (orzo)

[ ] Canned 4-bean mix (rinsed and drained)

[ ] Zucchini

[ ] Cherry tomatoes

[ ] Extra virgin olive oil

[ ] Garlic

[ ] Lemon juice

[ ] Fresh herbs (parsley, basil)

Method

Boil risoni in salted water until al dente; drain and lightly toss with olive oil.

In a skillet, sauté minced garlic, sliced zucchini, and cherry tomatoes until just tender.

Fold in the rinsed 4-bean mix and cooked risoni.

Season with lemon juice, sea salt, fresh black pepper, and chopped tender herbs.

Cook's Notes & Pairing Tips

Nutritious weekday meal prep staple; delicious served warm or chilled as a fresh pasta salad.
